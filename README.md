📊 Current Research

My current research examines:

🌽 Extreme weather, corn and soybean production, and export allocation in the U.S. Midwest
🌾 Flood shocks and rice trade in South and Southeast Asia
🌐 Climate shocks, agricultural trade networks, and market adjustment
📦 Trade, storage, and other mechanisms that may buffer agricultural production shocks
